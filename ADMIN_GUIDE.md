# Mount Excel Boarding School - Staff User Manual

**Administrator & Staff Portal Guide**

Welcome to the Mount Excel Boarding School Administration Portal. This guide explains how school administrative staff can log in, review student admission applications, record contact inquiries, and export student lists to Excel.

---

## 1. Accessing the Portal

1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Microsoft Edge).
2. Open the staff portal URL directly in your browser:
   ```
   https://yourdomain.com/admin/login.php
   ```
   *(For security and hardening, there is no public portal button on the website; staff open `/admin/login.php` directly).*
3. Enter your **Username** (or Email) and **Password**.
4. Click **Sign In to Portal**.

> **Note on Security:** For your safety, the system automatically locks an account for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive incorrect password attempts. Sessions will also log out automatically after 2 hours of inactivity.

---

## 2. Dashboard Overview

Upon signing in, you will see the **Dashboard Overview**:
- **Total Applications:** Cumulative count of student applications received.
- **Pending Applications:** Applications awaiting administrative review.
- **Accepted Students:** Number of students whose admissions have been finalized.
- **Unread Inquiries:** New parent questions or contact form messages.
- **Recent Activity Tables:** Quick preview of the 5 newest submissions.

---

## 3. Managing Admission Applications

Click **Admissions** in the left sidebar menu.

### Searching and Filtering
You can quickly find any student:
- **Search Bar:** Type the student's name, application reference number (e.g. `MEBS-2026-A8F2`), parent phone number, or guardian name.
- **Grade Filter:** Filter by specific class (e.g., `Play Group`, `Nursery`, `Grade 1`, `Grade 8`).
- **Status Filter:** Filter by processing status (`Pending`, `Reviewed`, `Accepted`, `Rejected`).
- Click **Apply Filters**.

### Updating Application Status Quickly
In the application table, each row has a status dropdown. Select the new status:
- **Pending:** Application received, awaiting staff phone call.
- **Reviewed:** Staff called parent, scheduled school visit.
- **Accepted:** Student officially enrolled.
- **Rejected:** Withdrawn, duplicate, or cancelled application.

The change takes effect immediately upon selection.

### Viewing Full Details & Adding Staff Notes
1. Click the blue **Details** button on any application row.
2. Here you can review:
   - Full student biodata (Nepali/English name, Date of Birth, Gender).
   - Parent / Guardian contact details and mobile number.
   - Permanent residential address.
   - Previous school attended.
3. In the right panel under **Status & Staff Notes**:
   - Write internal notes (e.g., *"Called parent on Sunday; birth certificate verified; assigned to bus route 1"*).
   - Click **Save Changes**.
4. To print an official summary sheet for physical student files, click **Print Application**.

---

## 4. Exporting Student Records to Excel (CSV)

School staff can export records at any time:
1. Go to **Admissions** or click **Export CSV** in the sidebar.
2. If you applied a search or filter (e.g., only "Grade 1" or only "Accepted"), the export will automatically reflect that filter.
3. Click the yellow **Export CSV** button.
4. Your browser will download a file named `mount_excel_admissions_YYYY-MM-DD.csv`.
5. Open the file in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets. All Nepali names and symbols will display properly.

---

## 5. Managing Contact & Inquiry Messages

Click **Contact Inquiries** in the left sidebar menu.
1. All public messages sent from the "Contact Us" page are listed here.
2. Unread messages are highlighted with a badge.
3. Click **Open** on any message to read the parent's full message.
4. When opened, the system marks the message as `Read`.
5. Click **Reply via Email** to open your email client and respond directly to the parent.

---

## 6. Updating Your Password

To maintain account safety:
1. Click **Account Security** in the left sidebar menu.
2. Enter your **Current Password**.
3. Type your **New Password** (at least 8 characters).
4. Retype your new password to confirm.
5. Click **Update Password**.

---

## 7. Signing Out

When you finish your work, always click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the sidebar or the red power button in the top right corner to prevent unauthorized access on shared computers.
